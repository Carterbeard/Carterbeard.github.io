About me

Hi, I am Carter — a Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence student at the University of Bath. I offer focused one-to-one tutoring designed to help students build confidence, improve exam performance, and understand topics in depth rather than simply memorising them.

I created this tutoring service to give students a calm, structured learning environment where questions are encouraged and subject-specific methods are taught clearly. Every session is adapted to the student's current level, learning style, and school exam board.
