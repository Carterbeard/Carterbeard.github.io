How to Book

Booking is quick and simple. Send one email and I will guide you through the next steps.

In your first email, please include:

  • Whether you are a parent booking for a student, or a student contacting directly
  • Subject(s), current year group, and target grade
  • A brief explanation of what support is needed
  • Your preferred lesson days and times
Send booking email →

What happens next:

  • 1
    Student information form

    I reply with a short form to understand the student's current position and learning needs.

  • 2
    Goals and structure confirmed

    We confirm current topics, goals, and the best session length for the student's situation.

  • 3
    Slot and payment details shared

    I share available session slots and send payment information.

  • 4
    Booking confirmed

    You receive a booking confirmation and everything needed for the first session.

Before your first lesson:

  • ✓
    Install Google Meet or Zoom and test your audio and video in advance
  • ✓
    Bring any class notes, homework, or topics you want to focus on
  • ✓
    Have a notebook or digital document ready for key takeaways
If plans change, please give as much notice as possible so the session can be rescheduled smoothly.
