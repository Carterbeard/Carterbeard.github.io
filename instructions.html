How to Book

Booking is quick and simple. Send one email and I will guide you through the next steps.

In your first email, please include:

  • Whether you are a parent booking for a student or a student directly contacting.
  • Subject(s), current year group, and target grade.
  • A brief explanation of what support is needed.
  • Your preferred lesson days and times.
Send booking email →

What happens next

  1. I reply with a short student information form.
  2. We confirm goals, current topics, and the best lesson length.
  3. I share available session slots and payment details.
  4. You receive a booking link and lesson confirmation.

Before your first lesson

  • Make sure your chosen platform (Google Meet or Zoom) is installed.
  • Bring any class notes, homework, or topic areas you want to focus on.
  • Have a notebook or digital document ready for key takeaways.

If plans change, please give as much notice as possible so the session can be rescheduled smoothly.
